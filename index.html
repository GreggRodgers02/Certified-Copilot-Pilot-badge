Certified Copilot Pilot Badge

Add the badge to your photo. Upload a headshot, nudge it into place, and download it — that's the whole thing.

Drag your photo here, or

Your photo never leaves your device. There is no upload, no server, and no account — the badge is placed on your picture right here in the browser.

Tips

  • A square photo works best, but any shape is fine — the sides are trimmed to fit the circle.
  • Drag directly on the preview to reposition, or use the sliders.
  • Use a photo at least 600 pixels on its shortest side so it stays sharp.
  • The download is a 750 × 750 PNG, sized for a profile picture in Teams, LinkedIn, or your email signature.

About the badge

The badge is a circular frame, not a solid graphic. A dark navy ring carries the words CERTIFIED COPILOT PILOT in white capitals. Gold aviator wings spread across the bottom of the ring, flanking a gold-rimmed medallion holding the Microsoft Copilot mark. The center is fully transparent, which is why your photo shows through it.
